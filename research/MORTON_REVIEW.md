# Review of the supplied Morton collection

Reviewed 23 September 2026, alongside [the DDOF review](DDOF_REVIEW.md). All 24 PDF pages were read, with rendered-page checks of pivotal equations and experimental descriptions. **This collection documents exploratory observations and a revealing partial replication; it does not demonstrate a gravitational force or a validated new force law.** It should not be treated as a controlled test of Brown's static solid-dielectric effect.

The filename *Morton Effect.pdf* does not identify one scientific paper. It is a scan compilation of Electric Spacecraft Journal material, including several authors and editorial comments. Its 2015/2016 file metadata are scanning/editing dates, not the dates of the experiments or publications. Exact file identity is recorded in [REVIEW_PROVENANCE.json](REVIEW_PROVENANCE.json); the original was not modified or added to the repository.

## The strongest finding is an apparatus control

In Yost's report, the assembly initially appeared to jump when a spark fired. A reference rod and magnified observation showed that it was gradually displaced before the spark, then recoiled toward its neutral position when the electrostatic loading changed (PDF p. 21, printed p. 16). Yost attributed the preloading to fields from the lead-in conductors. After insulating and grounding a shield around the extended lead, very little movement remained detectable (PDF p. 22, printed p. 17).

Other observations support sensitivity to the environment: a nearby hand increased deflection; inserting a Teflon sheet increased attraction; the wires moved as well as the assembly. Morton himself reports that approaching his device increased the reading to two or three pounds and explicitly discounts that as a false reading (PDF p. 23).

These findings concern Coulomb forces and mechanical recoil, rather than merely ionic wind. They demonstrate a concrete way to mistake an electrically loaded support's return motion for a propulsion impulse. Yost also observed ion wind, but judged Coulomb forces larger in his setup.

**Scope of the result:** the replication used a Wimshurst source and omitted the inductance in the initial setup (PDF p. 19). Morton had already said that a Wimshurst source did not reproduce some observations and emphasized resonant behavior. The report therefore cannot be presented as a definitive exclusion of all Morton configurations. It does undermine interpreting those particular uncontrolled deflections as new propulsion. It supplies no numerical upper bound on an unmodeled residual force.

## The proposed mathematics does not supply a usable alternative theory

Three checks can be made directly from the printed pages, without assuming Maxwell theory is complete:

1. **Force plus rate of force is dimensionally incomplete.** PDF p. 23 writes F = Eq ± d(Eq)/dt. The terms have units of N and N/s; a time scale is missing. PDF p. 2 instead adds a charge times a potential rate to an ordinary force, also without a dimensional bridge. The editors flag difficulties, but rewriting it as a Lorentz-like expression is not a derivation of a new law.
2. **The pulse-device arithmetic is wrong as printed.** On PDF p. 6, 66 pF × 100 kV is 6.6 μC, not 6.6 C. Likewise 10 kΩ × 66 pF is 0.66 μs, not 0.66 ms. These are factors of one million and one thousand respectively. A repetition rate also requires a charging/discharge model, not simply identifying it with 1/RC. These numbers were checked against the scan to exclude OCR error.
3. **The proposed value of G is a units-dependent numerical coincidence.** PDF p. 10 gives \(\epsilon_0^{-1}\sqrt{h/(2\pi c)}\). Its SI dimensions are \(\mathrm{kg}^{3/2}\mathrm m^{7/2}\mathrm s^{-2}\mathrm C^{-2}\), whereas G has dimensions \(\mathrm m^3\mathrm{kg}^{-1}\mathrm s^{-2}\). Agreement between numerical values in one selected unit system is not a physical relation.

The claims about gamma-frequency emission, negative mass, fusion and superluminal craft on PDF pp. 3–4 are extrapolations. No corresponding spectra, mass measurement, energy balance or propagation-time data appear in this collection. A de Broglie phase velocity is not a measurement of signal or spacecraft velocity.

## What the observations do and do not support

Schlecht reports sparks, audible pressure effects, motion of suspended balls, and partial illumination of a neon lamp. Those observations do not uniquely diagnose a new field; the document does not provide the discrimination necessary to exclude electrical pickup, charging, polarization forces, mechanical/acoustic excitation or gas motion. Spark color and lamp illumination do not by themselves distinguish ionic effects from a new interaction.

The powder reports mix charge retention, insulating surface films, liquids, evaporation, contact forces and thermal processes. The editor's oxide-film explanation is particularly relevant to the user's concern about electrical memory. It is a candidate ordinary mechanism, not a quantitative reproduction of every reported behavior. Rising particles or adhesion alone do not measure gravitational mass, especially where charge, flow or surfaces can support force.

There is no complete vacuum thrust protocol, raw force/time dataset, quantified uncertainty budget, matched dummy apparatus, or simultaneous closed-system momentum accounting in the supplied collection. Absence from this compilation is not proof that no additional records ever existed. The collection itself is insufficient to establish an anomalous force.

## Connection to DDOF and Brown

DDOF's NASA-proceedings citation turns out to include Yost's survey of the same ESJ research network, with explicit references to Morton (1991) and Schlecht (1992). That citation is a route back to these observations, not another independent experimental confirmation.

Most Morton devices described here use time-dependent high-voltage sources, sparks, resonances or RF excitation. The static solid-dielectric Brown hypothesis and the proposed asymmetric barium-titanate capacitor need their own assessment. Morton's reports neither establish nor rule out that effect.

The useful carryover is experimental: quantify lead and nearby-object forces, support loading and recoil, retained charge, voltage history, thermal motion and gas effects before interpreting a balance signal. For barium titanate, retain the polarization/strain/transport model in [MATERIAL_HISTORY.md](capacitor/MATERIAL_HISTORY.md); substituting it for PTFE does not by itself resolve electrical memory or generate the nonconserved source required by DDOF's corrected scalar branch.
